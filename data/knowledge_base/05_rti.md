# Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005

## What is RTI
Every citizen of India has the right to request information from a **public authority** — any government ministry, department, public sector undertaking, government-funded body, municipal corporation, panchayat, public university, etc. Private companies are not directly covered, but information about them held by a regulator can be sought from that regulator.

## How to file an RTI application
1. Write the application (in English, Hindi or the official language of the area) to the **Public Information Officer (PIO)** of the concerned department. You **do not need to give reasons** for seeking information (Section 6(2)).
2. Pay the application fee — **₹10** for Central Government bodies (by postal order, DD, cash or online). State fees vary. **Persons below the poverty line (BPL) do not pay any fee** (attach proof).
3. For Central Government departments, file online at **https://rtionline.gov.in**. Many states have their own RTI portals.
4. Keep a copy and proof of submission.

## Time limits for reply
- **30 days** from receipt of the application (Section 7(1)).
- **48 hours** if the information concerns the **life or liberty** of a person.
- 35 days if filed through an Assistant PIO; 40 days if third-party information is involved.
If no reply is received in time, it is treated as a refusal ("deemed refusal").

## Exemptions
Section 8 exempts certain information, such as information affecting sovereignty and security of India, Cabinet papers, information received in confidence from foreign governments, trade secrets, and personal information having no relation to public activity or interest. However, information that cannot be denied to Parliament or a State Legislature cannot be denied to a citizen.

## First appeal
If you get no reply, or are unhappy with the reply, file a **first appeal** to the **First Appellate Authority** (an officer senior to the PIO in the same department) within **30 days** (Section 19(1)). The appeal should normally be decided within 30 days (extendable to 45 days). No fee is charged for first appeals by the Central Government.

## Second appeal
If still unsatisfied, file a **second appeal** to the **Central Information Commission (CIC)** or the **State Information Commission (SIC)** within **90 days** of the first appellate decision (Section 19(3)).

## Penalty on PIO
The Information Commission can impose a penalty of **₹250 per day** of delay, up to a maximum of **₹25,000**, on a PIO who, without reasonable cause, refuses to accept an application, delays information, or gives false information (Section 20).
