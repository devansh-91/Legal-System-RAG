# Road Accidents, Traffic Fines and Motor Vehicle Law

## After a road accident
1. Get medical help for the injured — call **112** or **108** (ambulance).
2. The driver must take reasonable steps to secure medical attention for the injured and report the accident to the nearest police station within 24 hours (Section 134).
3. Note the vehicle number, take photographs, collect witness details.
4. Ensure an FIR is registered. The police must prepare a Detailed Accident Report and forward it to the Claims Tribunal.

## Good Samaritans
A **Good Samaritan** who helps an accident victim in good faith is **protected from civil and criminal liability** (Section 134A). They cannot be forced to disclose their identity or be detained at the hospital, and hospitals cannot demand payment from them before treating the victim.

## Compensation claims — MACT
- Claims for death or injury are filed before the **Motor Accident Claims Tribunal (MACT)** under **Section 166**, where the accident occurred, where the claimant resides, or where the defendant resides.
- Claims should be filed within **6 months** of the accident.
- **No-fault liability**: ₹5 lakh for death and ₹2.5 lakh for grievous hurt, without needing to prove negligence (Section 164).
- **Hit-and-run**: when the vehicle cannot be traced, compensation of **₹2 lakh for death** and **₹50,000 for grievous hurt** is payable from the Motor Vehicle Accident Fund (Section 161).
- Claims can also be settled at Lok Adalats.

## Common traffic offences and fines (central Act; states may vary compounding amounts)
| Offence | Section | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| Driving without a licence | 181 | ₹5,000 |
| Over-speeding (light motor vehicle) | 183 | ₹1,000–₹2,000 |
| Drunk driving (first offence) | 185 | Up to 6 months' jail and/or ₹10,000 |
| Drunk driving (repeat within 3 years) | 185 | Up to 2 years' jail and/or ₹15,000 |
| Not wearing seat belt | 194B | ₹1,000 |
| Riding without helmet | 194D | ₹1,000 and licence disqualification for 3 months |
| Driving without insurance | 196 | ₹2,000 (first offence) |
| Juvenile driving | 199A | Guardian/owner liable: ₹25,000 fine and up to 3 years' jail; vehicle registration cancelled for 1 year |

## E-challans
Challans can be checked and paid on the **Parivahan e-Challan** portal (https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in). Disputed challans can be contested before the traffic court / virtual court, and petty challans are frequently settled at **National Lok Adalats**.

## Hit-and-run under the BNS
Causing death by rash or negligent driving and escaping without reporting to the police or a Magistrate is punishable with up to **10 years** under BNS Section 106(2). (Implementation of this sub-section was kept on hold after protests by transporters in January 2024 — check the current status.) Causing death by negligence generally is punishable up to 5 years (Section 106(1)).
